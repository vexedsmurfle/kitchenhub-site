KitchenHub

A touch-first home hub for the kitchen.

KitchenHub is a home hub app that runs locally on a Windows touchscreen in the kitchen. It puts the things a household checks most often on one big, touch-friendly screen.

What it does

  • Spotify — music playback with transport controls, artwork, and volume.
  • Shopping List — a shared household shopping list, synced with Microsoft To Do.
  • Google Calendar — the household calendar, with events you can view, add, edit, and remove.
  • Today at a Glance — today's events, reminders, and tasks on one screen.
  • Blackboard — a shared board for notes and drawings.
  • Weather — current conditions and a short forecast.
  • Games and timers — family games and multi-timers for the kitchen.
  • Assistant — an AI assistant panel for the household.

Local by design

KitchenHub runs on the household's own Windows touchscreen. It is not a cloud service and it is not distributed publicly. It connects to the household's own Google Calendar and Microsoft To Do accounts only when the user signs in and gives consent, and sign-in tokens stay on the device.

Privacy Policy

KitchenHub — a private household app. This page exists to describe the app for the services it connects to.
