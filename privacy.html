KitchenHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

KitchenHub is a touch-first home hub app that runs locally on the household's own Windows touchscreen. This policy describes what it does with personal data.

Local by design

KitchenHub runs on your own device. It does not run on a server we operate, and it does not send your personal data to us. There is no KitchenHub account and no KitchenHub cloud service.

What it accesses, and why

With your sign-in consent, KitchenHub accesses only the services you connect, and only to carry out the actions you ask for:

  • Google Calendar — to read your calendar events and to add, edit, or remove events you ask it to. It reads only your primary calendar.
  • Microsoft To Do — to read and update your "Shopping List", so household shopping items stay in sync.

It does not access any other Google or Microsoft data.

Sign-in tokens

When you sign in to Google or Microsoft, the sign-in tokens are stored locally on your device, protected by the operating system's encryption. They are not sent to us and are not stored on any server.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store on a server, share, or sell your personal data.
  • We do not send your personal data to third parties, except to the Google and Microsoft services you connect, and only to carry out the actions you ask for.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
